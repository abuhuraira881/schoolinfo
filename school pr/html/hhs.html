Information About Happy Home School

Facilities

Happy Home School offers a comprehensive learning environment from early education to O & A levels. Equipped with spacious classrooms, modern science & computer labs, rich library, and dedicated arts & music spaces with physical sports facilities.

Policies

HHS follows a selective admission process with entrance assessments and interviews. Focus is placed on holistic development, academic rigor, character building, and regular parent-teacher collaboration.

Eligibility

Caters to children starting from Pre-School up to A-Levels. Eligibility criteria depend on grade level and age readiness, followed by an entrance test and parent/child interview.

ADMISSIONS PORTAL

HHS Admission Procedure & Policy

Step-by-step registration guide for Pre-School, Primary, Secondary (AKUEB & Cambridge Streams)

1
Online Registration

Fill out the online application form or obtain the official HHS Registration Form from the admissions office.

2
Document Verification

Attach candidate's B-Form, Parents CNIC, Vaccination Records, and Previous School academic report cards.

3
Entrance Assessment

Shortlisted students appear for a written entrance test evaluating English, Mathematics, Science & Urdu proficiency.

4
Parent Interview

Interactive interview session with parents and student followed by official fee voucher issuance.

HHS Mandatory Documents Checklist
Requirements for Registration & Assessment:
  • Photocopy of Candidate's Official NADRA Birth Certificate / B-Form.
  • Attested Photocopies of CNIC of both Parents (Father & Mother).
  • Photocopy of NADRA COVID-19 / Child Vaccination Card.
  • Two (2) recent official passport-sized color photographs with white background.
  • Character appraisal and fee clearance letter from candidate's previous school.
  • Original Report Card of previous academic grade.
Leadership Profile

Ms. Ghazala Nizami

Group Head & Pioneer

Mrs. Ghazala Nizami began her career teaching at HHS Cosy Corner School where she discovered her passion for education. Groomed by Mrs. Maryam Faruqi, she joined Happy Home School System and rose through the ranks. Mrs. Nizami is a staunch supporter of holistic education emphasizing problem-solving skills and hands-on experiences.

She developed the highly successful reading program CLOVER, a mandatory 20-minute daily reading period that enriches vocabulary and fluency. She also founded the HHS Fast Track School offering subsidized education to fight illiteracy.

Executive Leadership

Mr. Danish Faruqi

Executive Management

Danish Faruqi has been part of the HHS School System's management since 2015. He holds a Master's in International Education Policy from the Harvard Graduate School of Education and a Bachelor's in Economics from McGill University.

His vision for HHS is to continuously grow and evolve while instilling timeless values that empower students to serve Pakistan and humanity.

Financial Leadership

Ms. Rahma Nizami

Deputy Finance Director

Ms. Rahma Nizami holds an MBA and a Bachelor's degree in Science from McGill University. Joining HHS in 2011, she was promoted to Deputy Finance Director in 2016, managing operations and supporting women's empowerment initiatives.

Group Leadership

Nadia Mehmood Ahmed

Group Head

An alumna of HHS, Nadia holds a Master's in English Literature from the University of Karachi and a Gold Medal in B.Ed. With over two decades of experience, she works closely with Principals and Heads to foster continuous professional development.

Chief Operating Officer

Ms. Tehmina Khalid

Chief Operating Officer (COO)

Ms. Tehmina Khalid has over 22 years of dedication to educational progress. Heading the Quality Enhancement Cell (QEC), she oversees curriculum design, teacher training, and academic collaborations with institutions like AKU-IED.

Main School Campus

Happy Home School System provides a purpose-built educational environment equipped with modern infrastructure, spacious assembly grounds, and safe learning zones in Karachi.

Sports & Fitness Facilities

Physical education is deeply integrated at HHS, featuring dedicated spaces for indoor and outdoor sports, athletics, inter-house championships, and physical fitness activities.

Interactive Smart Classrooms

Classrooms are designed to encourage collaborative learning, equipped with digital boards, multimedia tools, and comfortable seating arrangement.

Science & Computer Laboratories

Equipped for practical physics, chemistry, biology, and computer science experiments, enabling hands-on technical skills for AKUEB and O/A level students.

CLOVER Reading & Knowledge Library

Central library supporting the CLOVER reading initiative, housing thousands of books, literature, and digital learning materials to instill lifelong reading habits.

Arts & Performing Arts Rooms

Dedicated spaces for music, fine arts, drama, and creative expression to support the holistic education model at HHS.

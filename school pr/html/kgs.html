Admissions Guidelines

Information About Karachi Grammar School

Facilities

Karachi Grammar School (KGS) is known for its world-class facilities, offering modern classrooms with digital tools, extensive libraries, advanced computer & science labs, sports fields, music & art rooms, and dedicated drama theaters across its campuses.

Policies

KGS follows a highly selective admission process focused on holistic education, encouraging excellence in both academics and co-curricular pursuits. Core values include discipline, social responsibility, respect, and active student empowerment.

Eligibility

Admissions are offered from Kindergarten through A-Levels with strict age criteria. Prospective students must undergo entrance assessment tests and interviews, with selection based on academic merit and aptitude.

ADMISSIONS PORTAL

KGS Admission Procedure & Policy

Step-by-step registration guide for Kindergarten, Junior, Middle & College Sections (O/A Levels)

1
Online Registration

Fill out the official online registration form or apply via the online modal during the annual admission window.

2
Document Verification

Submit attested copies of Child's NADRA B-Form, Parents CNIC, Vaccination Certificate, and School Transcripts.

3
Entrance Assessment

Registered candidates undergo competitive written examinations testing English, Mathematics, Science & Logic.

4
Interview & Selection

Successful candidates and parents attend a panel interview leading to final admission offer letter issuance.

KGS Mandatory Documents Checklist
Requirements for Admission Registration:
  • Photocopy of Candidate's Official NADRA Birth Certificate / B-Form.
  • Attested Photocopies of CNIC of both Parents (Father & Mother).
  • Photocopy of NADRA COVID-19 / Child Vaccination Card.
  • Two (2) recent official passport-sized color photographs with white background.
  • Character appraisal and clearance certificate from previous school.
  • Original Report Cards and transcripts of previous academic years.
Principal Profile

Mr. Carl P. Lander

Principal & CEO

Carl led prestigious schools in the UK and was recognised for developing academic excellence as well as building acclaimed extracurricular programmes. In 2013 he was recruited by the UK Government as an inspector and Director of Education for the British Overseas Territories.

Carl has over 25 years’ experience across primary, secondary and tertiary education. He served on the Board of Directors for the Council of British International Schools (COBIS) and is Chair of Synergy (Cambridge Community Schools group in Karachi).

Leadership Profile

Saadia Hannani

Assistant Headmistress

Saadia completed her schooling in the Middle East and holds degrees from the American University of Cairo and the Institute of Business Administration (IBA), Karachi. Prior to education, she worked in Investment Banking.

She joined KGS in 2004 as an A-Level Mathematics teacher and has held roles as Program Leader, Performance Improvement Lead, and Assistant Head of Student Services.

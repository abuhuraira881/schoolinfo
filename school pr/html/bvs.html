Admissions Guidelines

Information About BVS Parsi High School

Facilities

BVS is renowned for its comprehensive facilities, offering modern classrooms, science & computer labs, and specialized rooms for arts and music. Sports facilities include sports fields, a swimming pool, and a gymnasium for physical development.

Policies

BVS emphasizes academic performance, discipline, and character development. The school follows policies that encourage students to be respectful, responsible, and proactive in their studies with regular progress assessments.

Eligibility

Admission is offered from pre-school through A-Levels with specific age requirements. The process includes an entrance test and interview to evaluate academic aptitude and readiness for the respective grade level.

ADMISSIONS PORTAL

BVS Admission Procedure & Criteria

Structured admission policy for Grade 1 (Foundation) & Grade 6 (AKUEB / Cambridge Streams)

1
Registration & Form

Obtain the official BVS Registration Form. Fill in CAPITAL letters with blue ink or complete the online application modal.

2
Document File Prep

Punch all mandatory copies (NADRA B-Form, Parents CNIC, Vaccination Card, School Reports) into a Transparent Legal File.

3
Drop Box Deposit

Place the transparent file in a sealed Legal Envelope clearly labeled with Candidate Name & Grade, and submit in the School Drop Box.

4
Assessment & Interview

Shortlisted candidates receive admit slips for the Written Entrance Assessment followed by a Parent/Student interview.

BVS Admission Required Documents Checklist
Requirements for Admission Registration:
  • Photocopy of Candidate's Official NADRA Birth Certificate / B-Form / Juvenile Card.
  • Attested Photocopies of CNIC of both Parents (Father & Mother).
  • Parents Marriage Certificate photocopy.
  • Photocopy of NADRA COVID-19 / Child Vaccination Card.
  • Two (2) recent official family color photographs with white background (size 2.5" x 4").
  • Character appraisal and fee clearance letter from candidate's latest school.
  • Original Exercise books, Homework Diary, and Worksheets of previous class (English, Maths, Urdu).
  • Note: Child's surname must be clearly mentioned on all official NADRA documents. Single names are not accepted.

Historic Main Building

Established in 1859, Bai Virbaiji Soparivala (BVS) Parsi High School stands as an architectural emblem of heritage and academic excellence in Saddar, Karachi. The grand stone structure reflects over a century of educational distinction.

BVS boasts large athletic grounds dedicated to physical education, annual sports meets, cricket matches, and track & field activities.

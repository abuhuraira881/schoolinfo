Admissions Guidelines

Information About The City School

Facilities

The City School provides well-equipped classrooms, science and computer labs, digital libraries, and advanced sports facilities. The school is committed to fostering academic excellence alongside vibrant extracurricular activities.

Policies

Focusing on holistic development, policies balance strong academic rigor with character building, responsibility, and continuous monitoring through parent-teacher engagement.

Eligibility

Admissions are open from Early Years to A-Levels. Candidates are selected based on age criteria, academic potential, and success in entrance assessments & interviews.

ADMISSIONS PORTAL

The City School Admission Procedure

Step-by-step registration guide for Early Years, Primary, Secondary & O/A Levels

1
Online Registration

Fill in candidate details via the online admission portal or collect the form from campus administration.

2
Document Verification

Submit copies of candidate's B-Form, parents' CNIC, passport photos, and previous school report cards.

3
Entrance Assessment

Candidates appear for a written entrance assessment in English, Mathematics, and Science/Urdu.

4
Interview & Fee Deposit

Interactive parent/student interview session followed by fee voucher issuance for seat confirmation.

The City School Online Admission Application
Mandatory Documents Checklist
Requirements for Admission Registration:
  • Photocopy of Candidate's Official NADRA Birth Certificate / B-Form.
  • Attested Photocopies of CNIC of both Parents (Father & Mother).
  • Photocopy of NADRA COVID-19 / Child Vaccination Card.
  • Two (2) recent official passport-sized color photographs with white background.
  • Character appraisal and clearance certificate from previous school.
  • Original Report Cards and transcripts of previous academic years.
